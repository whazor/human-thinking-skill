# Human Thinking

A skill that leaves the thinking and writing to the human.

I tried various skills and planning modes. The AI provides good questions, but they tend to be multiple choice. So I made this skill.

I thought the questions needed to be hard. But the simple questions are often the ones that make you think the most.

## Thinking

The AI asks one simple, open question at a time, then waits for your response. Its next question follows what you say.

- No multiple choice or yes/no questions.
- No examples, hints, suggested answers, or answers of its own.
- Only the question, without explanations, summaries, or praise.

You find your own words and draw your own conclusions.

## Writing

The text needs to come from you. The AI can help organize it using only words you have actually said or written.

- It can delete text and move it around.
- It can reuse your text for headings.
- It can fix typos and speech-to-text errors.
- It cannot add its own wording or write new content for you.

## Use

Invoke the skill with `$human-thinking` and tell it what you want to think about. When working on a text, provide your own words and ask it to organize them.

## Installation

Clone this repository into your Codex skills directory:

```sh
git clone git@github.com:whazor/human-thinking-skill.git ~/.codex/skills/human-thinking
```
